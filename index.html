How We Can Support You

Voice Communications

VHF/UHF coverage across Cerro Gordo County using primary and alternate repeaters, plus simplex tactical channels.

HF / NVIS

Regional HF communications for coordination beyond local VHF/UHF range, including health & welfare traffic.

Digital Messaging

Winlink, FLDigi, packet, and other digital modes for resilient message handling when internet or phone systems are down.

Situational Awareness

APRS position tracking and trained net control operators for clear, organized communications during incidents.

Who We Are

Cerro Gordo County Amateur Radio Emergency Service (CGARES) is a volunteer group of licensed amateur radio operators dedicated to providing reliable emergency and public service communications.

We operate under the Amateur Radio Emergency Service (ARES) program of the American Radio Relay League (ARRL) and work in support of Cerro Gordo County Emergency Management and other served agencies.

Our operators maintain standardized readiness levels (Echo Scale), participate in regular training nets, and are prepared to deploy when conventional communications systems are overloaded, damaged, or unavailable.

How to Join CGARES

We welcome licensed amateur radio operators who want to serve their community through emergency communications.

Basic requirements:

  • Valid amateur radio license
  • Willingness to participate in training and weekly nets
  • Interest in emergency and public service communications

To get started, download and complete the official ARES Registration Form, then email it to the Emergency Coordinator at ec@cgares.com.

Weekly Nets & Training

Wednesday Nets – 7:00 PM

1st Wednesday: Simplex net on 147.570 MHz
All other Wednesdays: Repeater net on 147.15 MHz (TSQ 103.5 Hz)

Training Rotation

1st Wed – Weather Report
2nd Wed – Station Status Report (STATREP)
3rd Wed – Radiogram practice
4th Wed – Regular net
5th Wed – ICS-213 drill

Sunday Net – 6:30 PM

Alternate repeater net on 442.275 MHz (Tone 100.0 Hz)

Frequencies & Readiness

Primary Frequencies

  • 147.15 MHz (+600 kHz, TSQ 103.5 Hz) – Main repeater
  • 147.570 MHz – Primary simplex / tactical
  • 147.405 MHz – Alternate simplex
  • 442.275 MHz (+5.0 MHz, Tone 100.0 Hz) – Alternate repeater
  • 145.050 MHz – Packet / Winlink (1200 baud)
  • 3.5885 MHz USB – HF Digital (Olivia 8/500 / THOR 22)

Echo Scale

Current level: see live status

  • Echo 5 – Normal / Routine
  • Echo 4 – Elevated Alert
  • Echo 3 – Increased Monitoring
  • Echo 2 – Deployment Likely
  • Echo 1 – Full Deployment
  • Echo 0 – Recovery / Hot Wash

Echo level is separate from whether a net is running — a Skywarn or resource net can be active at any Echo level. See the CGARES SOP for full definitions.

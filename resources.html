Resources

Documents and pointers for CGARES members and served agencies. Prefer the SOP for local procedures; external links are public references only.

Core Documents

  • CGARES Standard Operating Procedures (PDF)
  • ARES Registration Form (FSD-98)

What You’ll Find in the SOP

  • Echo Scale and activation levels
  • Full communications plan (VHF/UHF + HF)
  • Net procedures and training rotation
  • STATREP format (Green / Yellow / Red station status)
  • Digital operations guidance (FLDigi, Winlink, APRS)
  • Common pro words

Message Forms & Traffic

  • ICS-213 General Message (FEMA blank form)
  • ARRL NTS 2.0 — ICS-213 / hybrid radiogram
  • ARRL National Traffic System (NTS) — radiogram practice and traffic handling
  • STATREP: use the Green/Yellow/Red format in the CGARES SOP (voice, FLmsg template from the EC, or APRStac)

Digital & Winlink

  • Winlink — email over radio for forms and reports when internet is unavailable (encouraged in the SOP; primary VHF data on 145.050 MHz)
  • FLDigi / NBEMS — see SOP digital guidance and the Iowa ARES digital net notes on Nets & Training

Iowa ARES & Skywarn

  • ARRL Iowa Section — ARES
  • Iowa ARES District 2 — coordinate through the section ARES leadership and your county EC; no separate District 2 public site is linked here
  • NWS Des Moines — Storm Spotting / SKYWARN
  • NWS Des Moines spotter registration

Ops Board

Ops board: dashboard.cgares.com — live weather, radar, and situational tiles for Cerro Gordo ARES. Net schedules, frequencies, and activation steps on this site and in the SOP remain the written reference.

Need something that isn’t listed here? Contact the Emergency Coordinator at ec@cgares.com.

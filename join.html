How to Join CGARES

CGARES welcomes licensed amateur radio operators who want to serve their community through emergency and public service communications.

Building the roster: We are welcoming new members as the CGARES roster refreshes around November 1, 2026. If you have been thinking about joining — or rejoining — now is a great time to introduce yourself and get on the nets.

Basic Requirements

  • Hold a valid amateur radio license (any class)
  • Willingness to participate in weekly training nets
  • Interest in emergency communications and supporting served agencies
  • Ability to follow standardized procedures and net discipline

What We Expect from Members

  • Regular participation in Wednesday nets when possible
  • Willingness to learn basic digital modes (FLDigi / APRS recommended; Winlink encouraged)
  • Maintain a go-kit or portable capability over time
  • Follow the CGARES Standard Operating Procedures

How to Get Started

  1. Download and complete the official ARES Registration Form (FSD-98).
  2. Email the completed form to the Emergency Coordinator at ec@cgares.com.
  3. Begin checking into the weekly nets.
  4. Review the CGARES Standard Operating Procedures.

After You Submit FSD-98 — Quick Checklist

  • ☐ Valid FCC amateur license (any class)
  • ☐ Radio capable of club primary frequencies (at least 147.15 MHz repeater with TSQ 103.5 Hz, and 147.570 MHz simplex)
  • ☐ Willingness to train on nets and learn basic digital tools over time
  • ☐ FSD-98 completed and emailed to ec@cgares.com
  • ☐ SOP reviewed (or bookmarked to read soon)
  • ☐ Plan to check into a Wednesday 7:00 PM net
  • Download ARES Registration Form (PDF)
  • Download CGARES SOP
  • Email the EC

Questions? Contact the Emergency Coordinator anytime at ec@cgares.com.

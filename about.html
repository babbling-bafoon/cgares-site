About CGARES

Our Mission

Cerro Gordo County Amateur Radio Emergency Service (CGARES) is a volunteer organization of licensed amateur radio operators dedicated to providing reliable emergency and public service communications when normal systems fail or become overloaded.

We support local emergency management, public safety agencies, and the community during disasters, severe weather, large events, and other incidents that require resilient communications.

Who We Serve

CGARES works primarily in support of:

  • Cerro Gordo County Emergency Management
  • Local public safety agencies
  • Community organizations during public service events
  • The National Traffic System (when needed for health & welfare traffic)

How We Operate

We maintain a structured readiness system (the Echo Scale) so our response can scale appropriately to the situation. Members participate in regular weekly training nets, practice formal traffic handling (ICS-213 and radiograms), and maintain portable and mobile station capability.

Our goal is to be a dependable, professional communications resource that served agencies can call on with confidence.

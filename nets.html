Monthly Training Rotation

Training items are passed as regular traffic during the net. There is no separate “training traffic” block.

  • 1st Wednesday (Simplex): Weather Report
  • 2nd Wednesday: Station Status Report (STATREP)
  • 3rd Wednesday: Radiogram practice
  • 4th Wednesday: Regular net (no specific training item)
  • 5th Wednesday (when it occurs): ICS-213 drill

How to Check In

When Net Control asks for check-ins, give your call sign phonetically, name, and location.

Example: “N0JBE, November Zero Juliet Bravo Echo, Justin, Mason City.”

Primary Frequencies

  • 147.15 MHz (+600 kHz, TSQ 103.5 Hz) – Main repeater
  • 147.570 MHz – Primary simplex / tactical
  • 442.275 MHz (+5.0 MHz, Tone 100.0 Hz) – Alternate repeater
  • 3.5885 MHz USB – HF Digital (Olivia 8/500 primary, THOR 22 secondary)
  • 3.970 MHz LSB – HF Voice (Iowa Traffic & Emergency Net)
